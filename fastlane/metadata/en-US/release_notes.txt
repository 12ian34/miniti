NEW

- Make your own templates. Settings, Templates: name it, add up to 8 sections with a line on what goes in each, preview it against a past meeting, then pick it from the More menu like BANT or SPIN. Duplicate a built-in to start, and export or import a template as a file.
- If miniti can't open your meetings it shows a recovery screen instead of crashing. Your data is never touched, and you can still export what it can read.

IMPROVED

- Security work on miniti's servers behind the scenes. Nothing changes for you.

FIXED

- When something doesn't save, such as a pin, a rename, or a delete, miniti tells you and offers a retry.
- If your history can't be read, miniti says so instead of showing an empty list.
- A correction that can't be saved keeps the editor open and tells you why.
