NEW

- Pin important meetings and browse history in clear Today, Yesterday, This Week, and Older groups.
- Import your Granola meeting history from its CSV export, with duplicate protection and clear source labels.
- Choose a Compact, Standard, or Large interface scale in Settings.
- Choose whether live transcript text appears on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island; it remains on by default, and tap the activity to return to your active meeting.

IMPROVED

- When several people are grouped as “Others” in Coaching, expand the filler section to see each person’s totals and filler-word detail.
- Clearer system typography and roomier transcript lines make meetings easier to read.
- On iPad, regular-width layouts use adaptive sidebar navigation while iPhone keeps its compact tab bar.
- Navigation and small utility surfaces use current system styling without changing Miniti's compact dark interface.
- Reduce Motion now applies consistently across app transitions and animated status elements.
- Starting, stopping, recovery, and post-meeting review explain what is happening and offer clear next actions.
- First-run setup is shorter, adapts to small screens, and includes a live microphone check.
- Insights use one descriptive mode menu with update timing, and Settings opens into searchable task-based categories.
- Summary, Questions, and Coaching stay prominent while optional Sales and Playbook views live in a specialist menu; Sales only runs after you enable it.
- Start meeting always begins a fresh meeting, while calendar meetings remain explicit choices in the upcoming list.
- Return to meetings and start another recording while final insights finish safely in the background.
- Transcript trims and speaker edits work with the system Undo command, while save, copy, and export confirmations stay inline.
- Consecutive transcript chunks from the same speaker appear as one clean turn with a single speaker label and timestamp.
- Meeting finalization is shown as non-interactive progress, with completion guidance only when its action is available.
- Longer meetings stay more responsive as transcription, coaching, rendering, and audio meters do less work on the interface thread.
- Saving and reopening long meetings avoids repeated full-transcript work, including while opening sheets or editing speaker details.
- Recovery autosaves run once per minute and skip unchanged meetings, while stopping, saving, and backgrounding still persist immediately.
- Managed Questions updates reuse prior context instead of repeatedly sending the full meeting as it grows.

FIXED

- Resumed interrupted meetings no longer restore unfinished or blank transcript fragments.
- Stopping or saving a short meeting no longer loses the last visible transcript when transcription disconnects before finalizing it.
