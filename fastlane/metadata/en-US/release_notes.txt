NEW

- On iPad, regular-width layouts now use sidebar navigation while iPhone keeps its compact tab bar.
- Summary, Questions, and Coaching stay prominent, while optional Sales and Playbook views live in a specialist menu. Sales analysis only runs after you enable it.
- Settings now opens into searchable, task-based categories, and first-run setup is shorter with a live microphone check.
- Start meeting always begins a fresh meeting; calendar meetings remain explicit choices in the upcoming list.
- Return to meetings and start another recording while final insights finish safely in the background.
- Clearer system typography, roomier transcript lines, and merged same-speaker turns make meetings easier to read.
- Transcript trims and speaker edits now work with the system Undo command, while confirmations stay inline.
- Reduce Motion now applies consistently across transitions and animated status elements.
- Longer meetings stay more responsive, autosaves skip unchanged meetings, and managed Questions updates reuse prior context.
- Starting, stopping, recovery, and post-meeting review now explain what is happening and show actions only when they are available.

FIXED

- Stopping or saving a short meeting no longer loses the last visible transcript when transcription disconnects before finalizing it.
