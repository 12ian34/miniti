miniti is an AI meeting assistant and speaking coach for iPhone. Record conversations with live transcription, get clear AI insights after every meeting, and improve your communication over time.

LIVE TRANSCRIPTION AND RECORDING
- Real-time speech-to-text while recording
- Automatic speaker labels so you can follow who said what
- Background recording support
- Dynamic Island and Lock Screen Live Activity recording status

AI MEETING INSIGHTS
- Structured summaries and clear action items
- Discussion flow, key decisions, and topic extraction
- Standard insights or MEDDPICC analysis for sales calls
- Regenerate insights anytime from meeting history

REAL-TIME SPEECH COACHING
- Filler word tracking with per-word frequency breakdown
- Talk ratio between speakers
- Pace (words per minute), longest monologue, and questions asked
- Clarity score to help you improve meeting by meeting

BUILT FOR REPEAT USE
- Full meeting history with transcripts, insights, and coaching metrics
- Search and review past meetings quickly
- Edit titles and copy transcripts/insights as markdown
- Stop and resume sessions without losing context

PRICING
- Free: 500 transcription minutes per month
- Pro: 5,000 transcription minutes per month for $4.99/month
- BYOK: connect your own Deepgram and LLM keys for unlimited usage from miniti at no additional app fee

SUBSCRIPTION INFO
If you choose Pro, payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel from your App Store account settings.
