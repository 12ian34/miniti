miniti is an AI meeting assistant and speaking coach for iPhone and iPad. Record a meeting, get a live transcript with names, clear insights as you go, and coaching that helps you speak better over time.

LIVE TRANSCRIPTION
- Real-time transcript in 11 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Greek, Polish and Russian
- Speaker separation, with real names worked out from the conversation and your calendar. Rename anyone yourself
- Optional on-device speaker separation on iPhone and iPad
- Keeps recording in the background, with a Dynamic Island and Lock Screen Live Activity
- Record from your wrist with the Apple Watch app. It transcribes on your iPhone

AI MEETING INSIGHTS
- Summary, discussion flow, action items, key decisions and topics, updated during the meeting
- Questions: smart things to ask next, based on what has been said
- Sales analysis with the eight MEDDPICC fields
- Templates for BANT, SPIN, interview scorecards, customer check-ins, stand-ups and 1:1s
- Playbook: answers from your own docs, with citations
- i zoned out: catch up on the current topic, questions aimed at you and recent decisions
- Investigate: look up a company, person or term from the conversation when you ask

SPEECH COACHING
- Filler words, talk ratio, pace, longest monologue, questions asked and clarity
- Trends across meetings, with examples from your own transcripts
- Optional live nudges when you talk too long or your filler rate climbs

CALENDAR AND MEETINGS
- Google Calendar shows what's next, with prep notes and join links for Meet, Zoom and Teams
- Auto-start and smart prompts when a meeting looks finished or the next one is starting
- Searchable meeting history: titles, transcripts, notes and insights
- Pin meetings, trim transcripts and regenerate insights
- Import your Granola exports
- Share as markdown or send to your own tools with webhooks

YOUR DATA
- Meetings are saved on your device
- Optional Cloud sync for Pro keeps meetings in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac
- Choose Cloud or Local for any meeting
- Settings, Privacy shows what leaves your device and who receives it

PRICING
- Free: 500 transcription minutes per month
- Pro: 5,000 transcription minutes per month for $4.99/month, plus Cloud sync
- Bring your own Deepgram and OpenAI keys: unlimited, you pay the providers directly
