miniti is an AI meeting assistant and speaking coach for iPhone and iPad. Record conversations with live transcription, get clear AI insights after every meeting, and improve your communication over time.

LIVE TRANSCRIPTION AND RECORDING
- Real-time speech-to-text while recording
- 11 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Greek, Polish, Russian
- Automatic speaker naming — miniti infers real names from the transcript, or you can rename any speaker yourself
- Background recording support
- Dynamic Island and Lock Screen Live Activity with optional live transcript text and tap-to-return

AI MEETING INSIGHTS
- Structured summaries and clear action items
- Discussion flow, key decisions, and topic extraction
- Summary insights, with optional MEDDPICC Sales analysis and a Docs-grounded Playbook
- Questions: AI suggests smart, context-specific questions to ask based on what's been said
- "i zoned out" — tap to catch up on the current topic, any questions directed at you, and recent decisions
- Regenerate insights anytime from meeting history

REAL-TIME SPEECH COACHING
- Filler word tracking with per-word frequency breakdown and custom tracking
- Talk ratio between speakers
- Pace (words per minute), longest monologue, and questions asked
- Clarity score to help you improve meeting by meeting
- Optional real-time nudges when you've been monologuing or your filler rate spikes

BUILT FOR REPEAT USE
- Full meeting history with transcripts, insights, and coaching metrics
- Pin important meetings and browse clear date groups
- Search meetings by title, transcript, notes, insights, or any content
- Import Granola CSV exports with duplicate protection and source labels
- Trim transcripts with Undo, then regenerate insights from the edited meeting
- Share transcripts and insights as markdown
- Compact, Standard, and Large interface scales, plus adaptive iPad sidebar navigation

PRICING
- Free: 500 transcription minutes per month
- Pro: 5,000 transcription minutes per month for $4.99/month
- BYOK: connect your own Deepgram and OpenAI keys for unlimited usage from miniti at no additional app fee
