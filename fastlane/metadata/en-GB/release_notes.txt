NEW
- miniti recognises you by voice. Turn on Your voice in Settings, Transcription, mark yourself once in a meeting, and from then on you are You from the first sentence, on the phone, in a room and in watch recordings. Needs Speakers set to this device. The fingerprint stays on the device.
- Remembered voices. People miniti has matched to a calendar guest are named next time before they say their name. Forget anyone in Settings, Transcription.
- Speakers get their email. A named speaker is matched to the calendar guest list; the email travels with the meeting in exports, the API and webhooks. Tap a speaker to see it or pick the right guest.
- Watch recordings get speaker labels from the on-device model when Speakers is set to this device.

FIXED
- A watch recording longer than 5 minutes failed with "Could not read the audio". Tap Try again on the row in History for a recording that was stuck.
