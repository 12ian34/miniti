- Live transcription now recovers on its own when it stops returning words mid-meeting, and keeps trying instead of giving up after the first few attempts.
- Recording recovers automatically after a phone call, Siri, or an alarm interrupts it, and after a failed switch between headphones and the built-in mic.
- Meetings no longer stop themselves with "no speech detected" when live transcription drops while people are still talking.
- A meeting that stopped because live transcription failed now says so, instead of reporting a silent room.
- A meeting you discard no longer reappears in your history, and deleting a meeting while its insights are being regenerated no longer brings it back.
- Training mode now counts "mhmm" as a filler word, and the default filler list no longer offers hesitations that could never be detected.
- The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island recording activity no longer risks crashing the app when its meeting is discarded.
- Suggested questions, docs topics, and calendar attendees can no longer be lost from a saved meeting.
