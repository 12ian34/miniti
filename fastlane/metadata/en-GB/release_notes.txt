NEW

- Separate speakers on your device. Settings, Recording, "Separate speakers on this device (beta)" runs NVIDIA's Nemotron speaker model on your iPhone instead of Deepgram's speaker detection. In our tests it got the speaker right for 6 to 20% more of the talking time, and it does best with several people in a room. Audio still goes to Deepgram for the words. The model ships inside the app, so this update is about 190 MB bigger. Off by default while it is in beta.
- Faster speaker naming and sales detection, in beta for managed accounts. Settings, AI, "Faster speaker naming and sales detection" matches speakers to your calendar attendees and spots sales conversations in under a second. Off by default.
- Calendar events with no guests and no meeting link are not meetings any more, so a personal block never auto-starts a recording. Change it in Settings, Calendar, Meeting filters.

IMPROVED

- Settings, About lists the open-source software and the speaker model miniti ships with, and their licences.

FIXED

- Google Calendar no longer shows as disconnected after a restart.
- The "has this meeting ended?" question now appears in a noisy room, stays up until someone speaks, and comes 2 minutes after a calendar meeting's end time.
