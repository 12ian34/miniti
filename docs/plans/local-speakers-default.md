## Failure handling fixes selected for 3.7.0

Source review: `OnDeviceDiarizationService.Session.process` marks a private `failed` flag and logs on inference failure; it does not notify AppState or invalidate the displayed readiness. `DeepgramService.makeListenQueryItems` omits the provider diarizer when a local timeline is active. Falling back to absent provider speaker fields is not restored Deepgram separation. The service comment and older archive's blanket fallback claim overstate current behavior; update the current reference alongside the fix, preserving historical evidence as history.

1. Surface model load, inference and sustained processing failure/lag through a bounded, generation-aware lifecycle signal. Do not let an old session's failure modify a replacement session or trigger an endless reconnect/reprepare loop. Stop feeding failed work and bound queued audio/memory; no per-buffer telemetry.
2. Before a Deepgram transcription session starts, if local separation is unavailable/preparing/failed, keep provider separation enabled under the existing cloud policy and report the actual route. Do not delay recording indefinitely for cold preparation. Validate the locally transcribed/private path separately; it must never upload audio as a consequence of this change.
3. In 3.7.0, do not initiate a reconnect solely to restore speaker separation after a mid-session local failure. Automatic mid-session restoration is deferred to the [next release after 3.7.0](../roadmap.md#next-release-after-370). Ordinary transport recovery remains owned by the recording reliability plan; this deferral must not disable it or introduce a second recovery owner.
4. Continue recording/transcription where possible with an honest speaker-separation-unavailable state after local separation fails. Do not invent confident speakers or claim recovery from connection-open alone. A local-only session stays local. A failed model must not remain falsely ready or repeatedly break subsequent recordings; bound cleanup/preparation and provide an explicit retry path for a subsequent recording boundary, without adding mid-session restoration to 3.7.0.
5. Cover cold launch and OS-update recompilation, missing/corrupt model, source changes, stop during preparation/recovery, failure on either source, late callbacks, user changes and private-mode boundaries. Keep manual identity corrections and voice-print learning safe when separation is degraded; uncertain/default labels must not become confident enrollment evidence.

## Local failure-handling implementation, 2026-11-03

Preparation and session ownership are generation-aware. A cancelled or timed-out load cannot publish progress/models into a replacement generation. Preparation has a 120-second soft deadline; an uncooperative native load must return before another load starts. A recording never waits for speaker preparation: cloud transcription retains provider separation unless every requested local source is ready. Local-only capture uses an unavailable timeline only when local separation was requested but unavailable. With separation off, it retains ordinary microphone IDs/names, reports `speaker_engine=unavailable` without a failure reason, and shows no failure banner. It never uploads audio for fallback.

The worker admits at most 128 packets total and eight seconds of queued PCM per source. More than eight seconds between fed and processed audio also fails separation. These are safety bounds, not measured M1 acceptance thresholds. Inference or lag failure rejects new input, invalidates the timeline and discards voice-collector evidence. Lag, including the Stop safety timer, preserves prepared models and ready status; after its worker settles the next recording can start without retry. Load/inference failures clear models and require explicit preparation. A late inference error can upgrade an earlier lag failure. Stop budgets two seconds plus queued seconds across the serial worker, counting the in-flight packet and the fed-minus-processed timeline gap for audio buffered inside the processor, capped at 18 seconds (two sources times eight seconds plus the tail allowance). A four-second backlog released after 2.3 seconds settles with reliable labels in the regression fixture. A native inference call cannot be forcibly cancelled; a worker still inside one prevents new local workers until it settles. Capture and cloud transcription continue independently.

After failure, later words use a fresh per-source unavailable identity with zero speaker confidence, bypassing the normal promotion rule that would otherwise retain the last named person. The app persists `Unknown speaker` through the existing speaker-name map, so saved meetings and other clients need no new document schema. Earlier identities and manual corrections remain intact. Automatic naming cannot replace this unknown bucket; voice matching/learning cannot consume invalidated evidence, including late embedding completions. The failed timeline remains latched across ordinary transport recovery, without a speaker-only reconnect or a second recovery owner.

The meeting shows separation unavailable. Settings offers explicit retry after load/inference errors before a later recording; session-only lag does not require it. Defaults and voice opt-ins do not change. Intentional discard invalidates and retires the old worker without fabricating an inference failure. New fixed speaker-state/failure events require managed mode and Share diagnostics, and exclude private recordings before either queue or local diagnostic timeline admission. Hardware snapshot production/storage/admin coverage is implemented locally, as described below.

Controlled fixtures cover cancelled preparation, partial-source load, inference failure on either source, bounded stalled work/finish, stale failure identity, unknown labels/manual correction, consent/private/BYOK gates and late voice embeddings. The full macOS suite and iOS compilation evidence are in the [release record](../releases/3.7.0.md). Physical cold preparation, missing/corrupt artifacts, dual-source capture, source changes and real-model throughput remain release checks. The opt-in WAV harness now feeds at capture pace to respect the bounded production queue. No physical or M1 result is claimed.

## Local hardware diagnostics, 2026-11-03

Mac snapshots use the existing managed, enrolled, Share diagnostics flow outside starting, recording, resuming and finalizing. Only coarse model/chip families, RAM band, support, preparation state/duration and thermal state are collected. Unknown and future identifiers stay unknown; raw sysctl strings never enter a payload. Status transitions and normal flushes sample at most six times per hour per process, suppressing repeated unchanged fields. The durable envelope omits meeting/diagnostic-session identity. iOS and Linux do not produce this Mac snapshot.

The backend accepts only proof-authenticated installation events and projects a hardware-only allowlist. Redis atomically retains the latest observation per canonical installation ID, rejects out-of-order observations and revoked installations, and expires the snapshot 30 days after observation. Installation/account removal clears it; a delayed retry cannot resurrect it. Consent off stops new collection and clears queued diagnostics, with already received snapshots subject to that retention. Existing bounded diagnostic history keeps its separate retention.

Admin rows/CSV expose the latest snapshot, while chip/RAM distributions count each installation once across casing aliases and label the consenting managed sample, unknown coverage and unavailable storage. Hardware does not change billing or authoritative usage. App Privacy details and website privacy copy are prepared locally; nothing is published.

Validation: 21 focused Mac hardware/disclosure tests passed; iOS generic build-for-testing passed. Backend fast suite passed 778 tests with five optional skips; the real Redis/Postgres lane passed 15, including latest-wins, observation-based expiry and revocation/replay. Current iPhone execution and physical Mac/model/thermal validation remain separate release checks.


Watch compatibility review (2026-11-03): the new bounded live worker exposed a prerecorded caller that queued an entire Watch file. It now awaits one-second chunks with a two-second processing bound and keeps provider labels on failure. Session IDs fence ingestion, live failure reporting and Stop/discard, preserving an independently owned Watch worker. The optional real-model voice-print harness uses the same backpressure path. Fourteen focused safety tests pass, including prerecorded timeout and stale-owner finalization; final combined/model evidence is in the release checklist. This does not change Watch billing, receipt, retry or cloud-transcription policy.


Real-model follow-through on M5 Pro (2026-11-03): all three opt-in tests passed using public AMI files. The diarizer processed 60 seconds at capture pace in 60.9 seconds with two detected speakers and lookup coverage 17/29; local ASR produced five finals, 13 interims and 52 words from that clip; voice recognition matched four speakers confidently across two eight-minute sections processed with bounded prerecorded feeding. This is functional fixture evidence on M5 Pro, not base-M1 performance, acoustic live capture, or a default-on approval. The full Apple suites subsequently passed on the combined tree.
