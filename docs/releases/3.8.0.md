## Decisions and preserved behavior

- Automatic Attio/Twenty sync runs once at first completion. Later edits/resumed recordings require manual resend.
- Setup preserves actual preferences. Audio ticks only after explicit review, verified email completes the account task, and completed recording/calendar prerequisites leave Home while retaining progress.
- Smart meetings stays on by default. Existing choices are preserved. Calendar automatic start is recommended after connection, never silently enabled. Local transcription and speaker separation are recommended separately; their defaults and Your voice opt-in do not change.
- Stop completes in place without Done. Durable recording, transcript, final insights, configured exports and accounting retain their existing owners. Private mode still permits configured CRM/webhook exports.
- Folders are flat, locally matched and editable, with manual overrides. Existing history changes only through Preview and Apply. Cloud remains optional and account scoped.
- First-use/sign-out account choice is explicit. Healthy enrolled users retain identity. Temporarily unreadable credentials cannot justify silent replacement.
- V2 keeps one recording identity across renewal and owner-bound replay. A complete successful legacy response may select legacy before capture; an established v2 session cannot downgrade. BYOK audio is not metered, but previously incurred managed usage still settles to its original owner.
- A genuinely timed-out audio worker stays unavailable until quit/reopen. The 15-second deadline counts execution, not queue wait. Received Watch results are cached before cancellation; lost provider responses cannot be reconstructed.

Ian confirmed both remaining choices on 2026-11-06: a continuing call stays in its current recording; new completed meetings file automatically, including for upgraders, while older history changes only through Preview and Apply. Calendar transition prompts require an explicit action; they no longer start a quiet-calendar handoff countdown. No larger ownership migration, automatic local-model defaults or new browser integration is included.
